# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Funders, institutional partners (foundations, embassies, corporates), school administrators, volunteers, and programme participants across Nigeria. Most arrive evaluating credibility: "is this organisation serious enough to partner with or fund?" Secondary audience: young people and community members looking for programmes.

## Product Purpose

tekforall.org is the public face of Tek4All, a Nigerian nonprofit bridging the digital divide through digital skills, AI literacy, and inclusive technology access. The homepage must read as a premium technology-for-development institution, not a charity template. Success: an international foundation officer lands here and immediately files Tek4All in the same mental bucket as a modern private-sector platform.

## Brand Personality

Calm, credible, institutional. Confident without being flashy. The visual system is Black / White / Steel / Matte Black: generous white and soft-white surfaces, matte black reserved for high-contrast statement sections, graphite headings, dark-steel body text, pale-silver hairlines, soft (never heavy) shadows. Gold exists only on the AI Readiness Quest surface (/quest); the homepage stays strictly monochrome.

## Anti-references

- Old NGO / charity-appeal websites (bright primary colors, crowded programme brochures, donation-guilt layouts)
- Government or NGO CMS templates
- Crypto / cyber / gaming aesthetics (neon, glows, dark-everywhere)
- Over-animated startup templates (bouncing, parallax abuse)
- Generic AI-generated landing pages (identical card grids, gradient text, eyebrow labels stamped above every heading)

## Design Principles

1. Whitespace is the credibility signal. Apple-level breathing room; density reads as cheap.
2. Matte black is punctuation, not wallpaper. Dark sections (pipeline, Sabitek, final CTA) earn contrast because the rest of the page is light.
3. Content is fixed; composition is free. Copy and imagery never change during design passes; layout, hierarchy, and rhythm carry the upgrade.
4. Motion is felt, not seen. Fade-up entrances, gentle image scales, soft card lifts. Exponential ease-out, no bounce, reduced-motion respected.
5. Editorial typography does the heavy lifting: strong scale contrast between display headings and quiet steel metadata.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Body text ≥4.5:1 against its surface (dark-steel on white, white/70+ on matte black). Visible focus-visible rings on all interactive elements. Buttons ≥44px touch targets. prefers-reduced-motion collapses entrances to fades/instant. Semantic landmarks and meaningful alt text on all imagery.
